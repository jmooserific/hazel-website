Support

Email me at john@hazelpuzzle.com. I read every message, and I'm always glad to hear from people.

If something's broken, tell me your device, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. The crash reports Apple sends me don't say who they came from, so I can't match one to your email. A sentence about what happened helps a lot.

Does Hazel need an account or an internet connection?
No to both. Everything happens on your device, so it works in airplane mode too.
Where do my photos go?
They stay on your device. There's no server for them to go to. The privacy page has the details.
What languages is it in?
English and Spanish. Hazel follows your device's language. If something reads wrong in Spanish, please tell me.
Can I send a puzzle to someone else?
No, and I'm not planning to add it. Sharing would mean keeping an eye on the pictures people send each other, and that isn't something I could do well.
Can I get more piece counts, other cut styles, other galleries?
More ways to cut puzzles is something I'd like to add. I'd rather not promise anything until it's ready, though.
